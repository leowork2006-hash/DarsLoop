# Product explainer

A captioned 100-second video is included at `public/explainer.mp4`. After deployment it is available at https://darsloop-production.up.railway.app/explainer.mp4. Its accessible text is at `/explainer-transcript.txt`, with matching captions at `/explainer-captions.srt`.

The video shows actual product screenshots with prepared fictional or authored source material. It is a screenshot walkthrough, not a staged fresh-upload benchmark or a student pilot. The one-hour result refers to one sequential synthetic repeated-English test; it does not establish native language accuracy or concurrent long-upload capacity. Automatic source-supported notes and practice are part of the app. Teacher check is optional.

The artwork, screenshots and captions are created for DarsLoop. The video is H.264, 1280×720, silent, with visible captions and an English subtitle track. No classroom recording, beneficiary voice, music, competitor artwork or private account data is included.

Registration concept and the September presentation predate the October implementation. Actual implementation and release history are disclosed in the submission materials; no history is backdated. Final contest submission and its receipt are separate from repository publication.
